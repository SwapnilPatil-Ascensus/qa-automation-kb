## Standard

Request access **before** cloning every repo. Confirm with your lead which environments you are allowed to touch (Stage 1 vs QC4 vs others).

Keep a personal checklist (not on SharePoint): Jira, qTest, GitLab, Jenkins, VPN, DB clients.
