### Intake Process

Supports Team Ravens’ mission by ensuring requests for maintenance, enhancements, fund changes, and integrations across Groups 1, 2, and 2.2 are handled efficiently.

- **Submit:**  Requests enter through a single channel with scope, urgency, and desired outcome.
- **Review & Prioritize:**  Items are assessed for impact, risk, and alignment with Uncork platform support and integrations.
- **Assign & Track:**  Work is owned, executed, and communicated through to completion.

### Onboarding Guide

Enables new team members to quickly contribute to Team Ravens’ support, enhancement, and integration responsibilities.

- **Start-Up:**  Access to tools, Uncork platform, and documentation.
- **Orientation:**  Overview of supported groups, partners, and core services.
- **Ramp-Up:**  Hands-on exposure to maintenance, document generation, and partner integrations.

### Escalation Path

Ensures timely resolution of issues impacting platform stability, clients, or partner integrations.

- **Team Level:**  Address within the Ravens team first.
- **Leadership:**  Escalate blockers affecting delivery or integrations.
- **Critical:**  Fast-track high-impact risks affecting Groups 1, 2, or 2.2.
