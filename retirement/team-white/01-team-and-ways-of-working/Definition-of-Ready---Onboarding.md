**Definition of Ready (DoR)**

- The actual user story written with this framework:
  - As a (Who?)
  - I want (What? What is the deliverable?)
  - So that (Why?)
- Story Title should not be generic; the objective should be clear/self-explanatory
- Acceptance Criteria
- Story is associated to feature
- Prioritized with MoSCoW
- Story follows INVEST
  - Independent
  - Negotiable
  - Valuable
  - Estimate-able
  - Small
  - Testable
- Discuss **high level**  technical approach as needed
- Story is pointed, making it clear that all team members understand the purpose
- Story is tagged correctly
