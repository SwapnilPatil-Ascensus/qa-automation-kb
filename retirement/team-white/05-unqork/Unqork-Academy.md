### Instructions for registering for the Creator Workshops

1. Register for a new account on Unqork Academy   [https://academy.unqork.com/web/en/sign-up](https://academy.unqork.com/web/en/sign-up)
  1. For the "I am" selection, choose "Unqork Customer/Prospect"
2. Login to your new account
3. Select Events -> All Events:
4. Find the Create Workshops you'd like to register for (for example, the one on 12/09) (note: I am in central time so you may see this slightly differently):
5. Click Register (I am already registered)
