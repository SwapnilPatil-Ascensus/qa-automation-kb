| **Ceremonies** | **Team 1** | **Team 2** | **Team 3** | **Owner** | **Participation** | **Activities** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ***Release Planning/PI Planning*** | <br>One single meeting together  <br>One month before the rele<br>ase start<br> | Product Owner  <br>Business | Product Owner  <br>Business Leaders  <br>SCRUM Master  <br>UX Team  <br>Technical Leads  <br>Architects  <br>Development Team  <br>QA Team | High level scope workshop EPIC/features/estimations  <br>Initial RAID discussions |  |  |
| ***User Story Refinement Sessions*** | Weekly Twice or more on need basis  <br>(60 minutes each) | Weekly Twice or more on need basis  <br>(60 minutes each) | Weekly Twice or more on need basis  <br>(60 minutes each) | Product Owner/BA | Product Owner  <br>SCRUM Master  <br>UX Team  <br>Technical Leads  <br>Development Team  <br>QA Team | Groom user stories ahead of sprint planning, keep user stories ready in backlog for next couple of sprints based on team velocity |
| ***Sprint Planning*** | Day 1 of Sprint   <br>(120 minutes) | Day 1 of Sprint   <br>(120 minutes) | Day 1 of Sprint   <br>(120 minutes) | Product Owner/BA | Product Owner  <br>SCRUM Master  <br>UX Team  <br>Technical Leads  <br>Development Team  <br>QA Team | Select Backlog Items for the Sprint  <br>Task Breakdown and Estimation  <br>Identify Dependencies and Risks  <br>Capacity Planning  <br>Acceptance Criteria |
| ***Daily SCRUM*** | Daily (30 Minutes) | Daily (30 Minutes) | Daily (30 Minutes) | SCRUM Master | Product Owner  <br>SCRUM Master  <br>Technical Leads  <br>Development Team  <br>QA Team | Brief on work completed previous day, work planning for current day and any RAID blocking the user stories |
| ***Sprint Review/Demo*** | One single meeting together  <br>Day 2 of new sprint for previous sprint (60 Minutes) | Product Owner/QA Team | Product Owner  <br>Business Leaders  <br>SCRUM Master  <br>UX Team  <br>Technical Leads  <br>Architects  <br>Development Team  <br>QA Team | Demonstrate Completed Work (Increment)  <br>Gather Feedback from Stakeholders  <br>Celebrate Successes  <br>Discuss Next Steps and Actions |  |  |
| ***Sprint Retrospective*** | Day 1 of New Sprint for closed sprint   <br>(60 Minutes) | Day 1 of New Sprint for closed sprint   <br>(60 Minutes) | Day 1 of New Sprint for closed sprint   <br>(60 Minutes) | SCRUM Master | Product Owner  <br>SCRUM Master  <br>UX Team  <br>Technical Leads  <br>Development Team  <br>QA Team | Discuss accomplishments, challenges and team Dynamics  <br>Review sprint metrics and performance  <br>Preventive actions for upcoming sprints |
| ***Scrum of Scrums*** | One single meeting together  <br>Weekly once (60 minutes) | Lead SCRUM Master | Product Owners  <br>Business Leaders  <br>SCRUM Masters  <br>UX Leads  <br>Technical Leads  <br>Architects | Team Updates  <br>Cross-Team Dependencies  <br>Discuss Blockers and Issues  <br>Coordinate on Shared Goals and Priorities  <br>Resolve Inter-Team Conflicts |  |  |
| ***Technical Workshops*** | On need basis  <br>Tech Lead may decide on frequency and occurrence | On need basis  <br>Tech Lead may decide on frequency and occurrence | On need basis  <br>Tech Lead may decide on frequency and occurrence | Technical Lead | Technical Leads  <br>Architects  <br>Development Team  <br>QA Team | Discuss on technical impediments |
