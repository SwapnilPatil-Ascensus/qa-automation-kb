(From Lynn Lehmann)

Richard, after our meeting this morning, I realized, I did not send this Friday.   I apologize.  I have added Geno for his awareness as well.   There are a few items we would like to add to roadmap for PAS as follows:

1. Combined Notices for plans that use the O’Neil notice delivery solution.  Kelly Strong and I spoke with Lori Jordan about this briefly, so she has some details, but essentially we are looking to combine the separate document notices produced by PAS into one .pdf. for plans that utilize the O’Neil notice delivery solution.  For example, Safe harbor and automatic enrollment notice or 403(b) universal availability and automatic enrollment.   This would be for new business/amendments, annual notice utility, and mass generation.
2. Participant Notice for non-ERISA 403(b) plans that will briefly describe plan provisions since there is no SPD or Summary of Plan Provisions for participants
3. Notice that will identify a change that is made when a plan is amended.   This would be for both internal and external use.
4. Plan Highlights document that would briefly describe plan provisions.
5. Snap-on amendment utility, that would permit a snap-on amendment and SMM to be produced in lieu of restating the entire plan document so that we could either choose a full restatement or just a snap-on amendment.
